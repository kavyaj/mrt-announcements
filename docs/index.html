MRT Announcements

Tap any station to hear its on-board announcement, followed by the doors closing. Pinch to zoom, drag to move around, and tap a line colour to follow just that line. The speaker button opens more train sounds, and the language button plays them in English, 中文, Bahasa Melayu or தமிழ்.

Map redrawn by hand for this page. Station announcements and train sounds are on-board recordings shared by WKX 076, plus a few of our own; a voice matched to the announcer fills in stations without a recording yet.

An unofficial fan project, made for a 3-year-old MRT fan. Not affiliated with LTA, SMRT or SBS Transit. The chime, door-closing sound and on-board announcements belong to their operators and are used here non-commercially.
